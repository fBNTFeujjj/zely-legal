## 01. INTRODUCTION

ZELY is an independent Discord bot designed to provide useful features and tools to Discord communities.

We respect the privacy of our users and are committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ZELY collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and how users can request its deletion.

By using ZELY, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this Privacy Policy.

## 02. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

To provide its functionality, ZELY processes and stores certain information obtained through Discord.

The following categories of information are stored in our MySQL database.

### 2.1 Discord User Information

- Discord User IDs.

These identifiers allow ZELY to recognize users and associate them with relevant bot functionality.

### 2.2 Discord Server Information

- Discord Server IDs.
- Server-specific configuration settings.

This information is used to identify servers and preserve their individual configurations.

### 2.3 Moderation Logs

ZELY stores moderation-related records generated through its moderation features.

Depending on the moderation action, these records may include:

- The Discord ID of the affected user.
- The Discord ID of the moderator.
- The type of moderation action.
- The date and time of the action.
- Relevant information associated with the moderation event.

These records help server administrators maintain moderation history and ensure accountability within their communities.

## 03. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING

The information collected and stored by ZELY is used exclusively for purposes related to the operation and improvement of the service.

These purposes include:

- Providing the bot's functionality.
- Identifying Discord users and servers.
- Saving and retrieving server configurations.
- Maintaining moderation history.
- Ensuring service security and reliability.
- Preventing abuse and unauthorized use.
- Diagnosing technical issues.

ZELY does not sell users' personal information.

## 04. DATA STORAGE

ZELY uses a MySQL database to store the information necessary for its operation.

Stored information is used to maintain user identifiers, server identifiers, server configurations, and moderation records.

Access to this information is limited to what is necessary for the operation and maintenance of ZELY.

Reasonable security measures are implemented to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## 05. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide ZELY's functionality and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods depending on their purpose.

When information is no longer necessary, it should be deleted or anonymized, where appropriate.

Users and server administrators may request the deletion of information associated with them or their servers through the official ZELY support server.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

## 06. DATA SHARING

ZELY does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be processed by technical service providers when necessary to operate the bot, including hosting and database infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

Only information necessary for the relevant purpose should be shared.

## 07. DATA SECURITY

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect information stored and processed by ZELY.

These measures aim to prevent:

- Unauthorized access.
- Accidental loss or destruction.
- Unauthorized modification.
- Unlawful disclosure.
- Other forms of misuse.

However, no online service or database can guarantee absolute security.

## 08. USER PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on applicable data protection legislation, including the GDPR, users may have the right to:

- **Access:** Request access to their personal information.
- **Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate information.
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of their personal information.
- **Restriction:** Request restrictions on certain processing activities.
- **Objection:** Object to certain processing activities.
- **Data portability:** Request a copy of eligible personal information.

To exercise these rights, users can contact the ZELY team through the official support server.

All requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
